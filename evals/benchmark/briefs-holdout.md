# Holdout briefs (added in round 10; the skill was never tuned on these)

## B4 - launch.pptx
Product-launch pitch to the 25-person sales team of "Fabrikam Analytics" for its new product "Fabrikam Insight Pro",
launching 1 March 2027. 8-10 slides. Data: list price 49 USD per user per month (annual plan 470 USD per user per
year); pilot results with 12 beta customers: report-building time down from 6.5 to 2.0 hours per week, 10 of 12 beta
customers converted to paid; target 120 new customers and 1.8 MUSD new annual recurring revenue in 2027; three main
competitors priced at 59, 65 and 79 USD per user per month; launch incentive: 15 % off the first year for deals signed
by 30 April 2027; extra sales commission of 2 % on Insight Pro deals in Q1-Q2. Cover: the customer problem, what is
new, proof from the pilot, pricing vs competitors, the target, objection handling (top 3 objections), what each
salesperson should do in the first 30 days.

## B5 - postmortem.pptx
Project post-mortem for engineering leadership (CTO and 6 engineering managers) on the "Payments Platform v2"
migration. 7-9 slides. Data: planned 6 months / 1.2 MSEK budget, actual 9 months / 1.65 MSEK; scope 14 services
migrated (all 14 delivered); incidents during migration: 3 customer-facing, the longest 47 minutes; p95 payment
latency 820 ms -> 310 ms after migration; infrastructure cost 92 kSEK -> 68 kSEK per month. Root causes of the
delay: underestimated data migration (8 weeks over), a vendor API change (3 weeks), two engineers moved to another
project in month 4. Cover: summary, what went well, what went wrong, timeline plan vs actual, root causes, lessons
learned, and 4 concrete actions for the next project with owners. Blameless tone.
