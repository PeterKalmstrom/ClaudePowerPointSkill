# Benchmark briefs (same for every arm)

## B1 - qbr.pptx
Quarterly business review for "Northwind Cloud", a B2B SaaS company, for the executive team. 8-10 slides.
Data: revenue Q1-Q4 2026: 4.1, 4.6, 5.2, 5.9 MUSD; net revenue retention 112 %; churn 1.8 %/month -> 1.2 %/month;
new customers per quarter 38, 41, 52, 60; top 3 risks: enterprise sales cycle lengthening, one data-centre outage
in Q3, hiring behind plan (14 of 20). Ask: approve 3 extra sales engineers.

## B2 - phishing.pptx
Phishing-awareness training for all employees of a 300-person company, presented in a 20-minute session. 7-9
slides. Cover: what phishing is, five red flags, a realistic example email, what to do (report button, don't click,
call IT), what happens after you report, a short quiz slide. Friendly, practical tone.

## B3 - fourday.pptx
Proposal to the board: a 6-month four-day-week pilot for the 40-person engineering department. 7-9 slides.
Include: why (retention, burnout survey: 41 % report burnout), pilot design, timeline (Jan-Jun with milestones),
success metrics, risks and mitigations, cost table (overtime buffer 120 kSEK, tooling 30 kSEK, evaluation 50 kSEK),
the decision asked for.
