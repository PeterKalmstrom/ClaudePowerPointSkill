Score every deck from 1 to 10 on six criteria:
- message: clear story and claim, faithful to the brief, ends on what the audience must do or decide
- design: visual quality, consistency, appropriate visuals
- legibility: readable when projected (type size, contrast, density)
- layout: nothing overlapping, cut off, misaligned or awkwardly empty
- data: figures correct against the brief (check arithmetic), charts right for the data and labelled
- notes: presenter support - speaker notes a presenter could actually use to deliver the talk

Write the result as JSON to <BENCH>/judge/roundN-scores.json in exactly this shape: {"P": {"qbr": {"message":n,"design":n,"legibility":n,"layout":n,"data":n,"notes":n,"comment":"one or two sentences citing slide numbers"}, "phishing": {...}, "fourday": {...}, "mean": {"message":x,"design":x,"legibility":x,"layout":x,"data":x,"notes":x,"overall":x}}, "Q": {...}, "R": {...}}. Means are over the three decks (2 decimals), overall is the mean of the six criterion means. Write only that file. Then reply with a short summary per maker: strengths, weaknesses, and which single deck was best overall and why.
